# HR Analytics Dashboard using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee data and understanding employee attrition using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides insights into workforce demographics, employee turnover, salary distribution, business travel, and employee tenure.

HR Analytics helps organizations understand employee behavior, identify attrition patterns, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee data and workforce demographics
- Calculate key HR metrics such as total employees, attrition count, and attrition rate
- Analyze employee attrition across different age groups
- Understand attrition patterns based on salary slabs
- Compare employee attrition across departments
- Analyze attrition by gender, marital status, and education field
- Evaluate the relationship between employee tenure and attrition
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Provide insights to support employee retention strategies

## 📊 Dashboard

### 1. HR Overview and Key Performance Indicators

This section provides an overview of the workforce and important HR metrics.

Key metrics include:

- Count of Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Salary
- Average Tenure

Interactive filters include:

- Age Group
- Education Field
- Department

### 2. Employee Attrition Analysis

This section analyzes employee attrition across different categories.

Visualizations include:

- Attrition Count by Salary Slab
- Attrition Count and Rate by Age Group
- Attrition Count by Business Travel
- Attrition Count by Department

These visualizations help identify workforce categories with higher attrition and areas that may require further investigation.

### 3. Employee Demographics Analysis

This section explores attrition across employee demographic categories.

Visualizations include:

- Attrition Count by Gender
- Attrition by Marital Status
- Attrition by Education Field

These insights help HR teams understand how attrition is distributed across different employee groups.

### 4. Employee Tenure Analysis

This section examines employee attrition in relation to years spent at the company.

Visualization:

- Attrition by Years at Company

This analysis helps identify tenure levels where employee attrition is concentrated and where retention initiatives may be beneficial.

## 📈 Key HR Metrics

### Employee Count

Represents the total number of employees included in the dataset.

### Attrition Count

Represents the number of employees who have left the organization, according to the dataset.

### Attrition Rate

Measures the percentage of employees who have left the organization relative to the total employee count.

### Average Age

Represents the average age of employees in the dataset.

### Average Salary

Represents the average salary of employees.

### Average Tenure

Represents the average number of years employees have worked at the company.
